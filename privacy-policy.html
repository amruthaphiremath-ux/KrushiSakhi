What data we collect

The app does not require login and does not collect personal information such as name, email, or location. The only data submitted is the crop name and number of months you choose, sent to our server solely to generate a price prediction.

How predictions are generated

Predictions are produced by a machine learning model (LSTM) trained on historical crop price and rainfall data. Predictions are estimates only and should not be treated as financial or agricultural advice.

Third-party services

The app loads the Chart.js library from a public content delivery network (cdnjs.cloudflare.com) to render price-trend graphs. No personal data is shared with this or any other third party.

Data storage

We do not store or retain any prediction requests beyond what is needed to generate and return the result.
